## Show Notes

Thousands of open purchase orders. Hundreds of suppliers. And a buying team chasing confirmations one email at a time. If that sounds like your procurement department, you're not alone.

In episode 2, Tal Rozenberg, CEO of RH Group, shared how AI agents from a company called Evolinq cut his buyers' time on day-to-day follow-ups by more than 60%, according to RH Group. So we went straight to the source.

Noam Shakuri is co-founder and CEO of Evolinq, which builds AI agents that work as digital buyers for manufacturers. Before Evolinq, he spent more than a decade leading engineering teams in cybersecurity and risk intelligence. In January 2026, TechCrunch named Evolinq one of the top logistics and manufacturing startups from its Startup Battlefield.

## In This Episode

- Why procurement at even the most advanced manufacturers still runs on email and spreadsheets, and what happens when one order slips through
- The two bad choices buyers are left with when they can't keep up
- How an AI agent learns your buyers' work just by being copied on their emails, without replacing your ERP
- The early warning signs hiding in your suppliers' replies, from slower responses to changes in tone
- The mistake most manufacturers make when they prepare for AI, and why you don't need to rebuild your infrastructure first

You don't need a data science team or a new ERP to take the busywork out of procurement. You need to start with the process your buyers already run.
