## Show Notes

Your AI pilots worked. Your P&L didn't move.

Most companies now have dozens of small AI experiments and very little to show for them at the operations level. Sri Ambati, Founder and CEO of H2O.ai, has watched that pattern across many large and mid-sized companies. In this conversation with Dr. Petra Doerken he explains what the companies that scale AI do differently, and why it starts with the CEO.

## In This Episode

- Why a handful of quick wins rarely changes how a company runs
- Why the CEO has to use AI personally, not delegate it
- How to pick a problem that is worth the cost of solving
- What mid-sized manufacturers can realistically do without a data science team
- Why writing down intent and simplifying processes comes before adding AI agents
