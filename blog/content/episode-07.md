## Show Notes

Margins are shrinking, the board wants answers, and the obvious move is to start cutting. Before you do, hear from a CEO who has led manufacturers out of crisis more than once.

Serge Joris took the helm at Frigoglass, a manufacturer of coolers for some of the world’s most iconic beverage brands, in 2024 while the company was in deep crisis. In 2025 it posted record EBITDA, more than three times the year before. Before that, he led Barcelona’s Girbau Group through the pandemic and spent 15 years running businesses inside Dover Corporation, which acquired the software company he founded.

In this conversation with Dr. Petra Doerken, Serge walks through the playbook he uses to transform a company, and explains why a transformation should never be declared finished.

## In This Episode

- Why he spends his first 90 days in the field instead of in the numbers, and why he meets with his competitors
- How to turn 150 ideas from your team into five or six initiatives that actually move the needle
- Why he removes the amber light from every performance dashboard
- Why cutting costs can destroy the muscle you need to recover, and what he did instead
- What it takes to turn an equipment manufacturer into a business that sells data and services

You don’t need a crisis to transform. You need the discipline to start before one finds you.
