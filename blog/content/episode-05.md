## Show Notes

When a part goes missing in your warehouse, the cost doesn’t stay in the warehouse. It shows up as a stopped production line, a late shipment or a lost customer. Yet most manufacturers only learn the true state of their inventory when they count it, often months apart, and make decisions in between on numbers they assume are right.

Sankalp Arora, co-founder and CEO of Gather AI, argues those numbers often aren’t. His company uses drones and cameras on forklifts to show operations leaders what is actually on their shelves and moving through their facilities, and the gap between what the KPIs say and what’s really there is frequently a surprise.

We heard about Gather AI in our last episode, when Pano Anthos of XRC Ventures mentioned it as one of his portfolio companies. We were curious, so we invited Sankalp to tell us more.

Sankalp holds a PhD in robotics from Carnegie Mellon University, where he and his co-founder developed an autonomous helicopter for DARPA, the research agency of the US Department of Defense. Seeing the detailed 3D map that helicopter built of everything around it led him to found Gather AI.

## In This Episode

- What manufacturers lose to inventory they can’t find, from missed sales to production line stoppages
- Why physical data, information that exists on your floor but was never digitized, is a major untapped opportunity
- What a deployment looks like, from mapping a facility to the first surprising inventory numbers
- How to move from reacting to problems to preventing them, including how one 3PL cut customer complaints by 60%
- When a mid-market operation is ready for physical AI, and when it should get organized first
- How AI agents are moving from surfacing insights to recommending actions a manager can approve
- Why most AI pilots fail before they start, and what to agree on up front
- His contrarian take on what the industry gets wrong about IT, integration costs and investment
- Why he believes leaders must help spread AI skills widely to prevent power from concentrating

If you run a manufacturing, industrial or logistics operation and suspect your numbers aren’t telling the whole story, this episode shows what becomes possible when you can see your operation as it really is.
