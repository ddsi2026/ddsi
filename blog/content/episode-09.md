## Show Notes

Every manufacturer is being told to bring in AI and robots. Few are told how to do it without putting the plant, or their people, at risk.

Tim Draper is a third-generation venture capitalist who has backed companies including Tesla, SpaceX, Skype, Baidu, Coinbase and Robinhood, and who coined the term "viral marketing" while funding Hotmail. Today he is backing companies that build in the physical world, from portable nuclear reactors to a new kind of aircraft. In this conversation with Uwe Doerken, former Chairman and CEO of DHL, Tim turns what he sees from the venture side into practical advice for manufacturing leaders.

## In This Episode

- The simple test Tim uses to tell whether an industry is ripe for disruption
- Why he'd "try before you buy" every AI pitch, and where AI isn't reliable enough for your production line yet
- When to build AI in-house and when to lean on the suppliers you already use
- Two ways to bring robots onto the shop floor without betting the plant
- The one thing to get right first, and why your team will resist AI if you skip it

You don't have to bet the plant to get started. You have to bring your people with you.
