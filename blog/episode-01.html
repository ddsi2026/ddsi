The Transformation Playbook From the Man Who Led the World's Largest Express Company | Uwe Doerken

2027-01-01
Guest: Uwe Doerken, Former CEO and Executive Chairman, DHL Worldwide Express

Show Notes

Most transformation programs are built to satisfy a board. Uwe Doerken argues they should be built around one measurable benefit to the customer. As former CEO and Executive Chairman of DHL Worldwide Express and a 15-year member of the Deutsche Post World Net executive board, Uwe led more than 100 acquisitions and organizational change across 200+ countries. Today he invests in and operates businesses through a family office, with a strong focus on AI.

In This Episode

  • Why a single customer metric, like next-day letter delivery in Germany, can drive an entire company's transformation
  • The board structure he used at DHL to balance running the business with changing it
  • Why tracking AI usage (inputs) is not the same as productivity and customer value (outputs)
  • Why the real risk with AI is not thinking radically enough
  • Why most M&A deals fail in post-merger integration, not in the deal itself
  • How "strategic degrees of freedom" help startups and leaders pivot without losing focus
  • Why waiting to be a fast follower on AI may be the riskier choice

If you lead a company through technology adoption or operational change, this conversation gives you a practical framework covering structure, leadership, culture, and customer focus.
