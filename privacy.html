Privacy Policy

Updated January 1, 2026

D&D Strategic Impact, part of the D&D Legacy Capital family office, respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

We collect names, email addresses, and phone numbers when you engage with our website or services, such as through our contact form. We also gather basic device information to improve the site experience.

How We Use Your Data

Collected information helps us personalize your experience, improve our website, respond to inquiries, process the requests you submit, and, where you've opted in, send periodic communications.

Third-Party Sharing

We may share information we collect, both personal and non-personal, with service providers who support our operations (such as our form-processing and email providers). We may disclose information to law enforcement when legally required. We do not sell your personal information.

Security Measures

We use SSL encryption for sensitive transactions and maintain physical, electronic, and managerial safeguards. That said, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and neither people nor security systems are foolproof.

Your Rights

You can request that we update, correct, or delete your information by reaching out through our contact form. You can also unsubscribe from any email communications at any time.

International Data Transfer

As a global organization, information you provide may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own.

Regulatory Compliance

This policy is intended to meet the requirements of the GDPR for EU residents and the CCPA/CalOPPA for California residents. We do not sell personal information.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
