Global Gateway Incubator 2027 Cohort

A 16-week incubator (January–April 2027) powered by D&D Legacy Capital in direct partnership with 5 premier universities across India and Vietnam. Student founders undergo an intensive business and technical-architecture transformation, equipping them to acquire US/EU enterprise customers, raise institutional funding, and target global exits, entirely free of cost.

The program runs on a hybrid cadence of bi-weekly masterclasses with global VC investors and enterprise executives, bi-weekly cross-university peer working sessions, and dedicated 1-on-1 architecture advisory from DDSI's in-house technology team. In April, the top 5 highest-execution teams pitch live to an exclusive group of international investors and enterprise partners at Demo Day.
