The Opportunity & Vision

Talent is universal. Opportunity isn't.

Talent is universally distributed, but opportunity and venture capital remain concentrated. Powered by D&D Legacy Capital in direct partnership with 4 premier universities across India and Vietnam, the Global Gateway Incubator bridges this gap. Attending completely free of cost, student founders undergo an intensive 16-week business and architectural transformation. We expand their horizons beyond local markets, equipping them to acquire US/EU enterprise customers, raise institutional funding, and target global exits.

Program Delivery Model

16-Week Continuous Hybrid Cadence

Bi-Weekly Masterclasses
Global VC investors, enterprise executives, and scale-up experts host live interactive talks on key venture pillars.
Bi-Weekly Peer Working Sessions
Cross-university cohort workshops where teams present progress, share lessons, and pressure-test strategy.
Dedicated Architecture Advisory
Our in-house technology team provides direct one-on-one offline and live technical reviews to refine system architecture and security.
Global Speakers & Advisory Network
Students are introduced to a global network of operators, rather than theorists, who have successfully built and scaled companies.
